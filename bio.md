I am a PhD engineer with deep expertise in fluid dynamics, simulation, and data-driven engineering. Over the past decade, I have contributed to projects with a focus on aerodynamic optimization, flow control, computational design, and multi-physics modeling.

I recently completed a Master's degree in Data Science with a concentration in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering from <a href="https://degrees.apps.asu.edu/masters-phd/major/ASU00/ESDSEMAEMS/data-science-analytics-and-engineering-mechanical-and-aerospace-engineering-ms?init=false&nopassive=true">Arizona State University</a>. This allowed me to combine my background in experimental and numerical fluid dynamics with modern machine learning techniques and AI agent workflows to optimize complex systems. My work integrates CFD, wind tunnel testing, and ML/AI-driven automation to drive performance improvements and energy efficiency.

Currently, I am a Senior Engineer at [CesiumAstro](https://www.cesiumastro.com/), where I build automated CAD pipelines using Siemens NX that programmatically construct, modify, and evaluate 3D assemblies for aerospace propulsion hardware. I develop parametric geometry workflows for simulation-in-the-loop design optimization and create Python-based automation scripts for batch geometry processing and design-space exploration. I leverage AI coding agents daily for CAD scripting, simulation automation, and engineering workflow optimization.

Previously, I led simulation and R&D teams at [MATTUR](https://www.mattur.com/) and [SMOORE International](https://en.smooreholdings.com/), where I developed ML-based control strategies and created reduced-order models for complex turbulent flows. I also designed neural networks that act like an Engine Control Unit and govern the functioning of combustion engines, as well as AI-based pitch control for wind turbines.

Before that, I worked at the [DynFluid Laboratory](https://dynfluid.ensam.eu/) in Paris, France, where I focused on numerical simulations and data-driven methods for fluid dynamics. Prior to that, I was at the [Institut AéroTechnique](https://iat.cnam.fr/), conducting experimental wind tunnel testing and fluid simulations. I hold a granted European patent for an annular housing optimized for horizontal axis wind turbines.

I'm passionate about applying smart algorithms to physical systems, bridging the gap between theory, computation, and real-world testing.
