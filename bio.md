Dan Hlevca is a Senior Mechanical Engineer specializing in **Fluid Dynamics & Machine Learning** at [CesiumAstro](https://cesiumastro.com/) in El Segundo, CA. He holds a Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering and an M.S. in Data Science (ML/AI) from Arizona State University.

His research focuses on:
- **Aerodynamic optimization** and flow control
- **Machine learning** applications in fluid dynamics
- **CFD simulations** and data-driven modeling
- **Wind energy** systems and turbine design

Dan has published multiple papers in prestigious journals including *AIAA*, *Experiments in Fluids*, and *Journal of Wind Engineering and Industrial Aerodynamics*. His work combines experimental fluid dynamics with modern machine learning techniques to solve complex engineering problems.

When he's not engineering, Dan enjoys working on personal projects that bridge the gap between traditional fluid dynamics and cutting-edge AI technologies.
