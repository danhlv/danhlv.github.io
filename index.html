I am a PhD engineer with a deep expertise in fluid dynamics, simulation, and data-driven engineering. Over the past decade, I have contributed to projects, with a focus on aerodynamic optimization, flow control, and multi-physics modeling.

Currently pursuing a Master’s degree in Data Science with a concentration in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at Arizona State University. I am combining my background in experimental and numerical fluid dynamics with modern machine learning techniques to optimize complex systems. My work integrates CFD, wind tunnel testing, and ML/AI to drive performance improvements and energy efficiency.

Professionally, I have led simulation and R&D teams at companies like MATTUR and SMOORE International, where I developed ML-based control strategies and created reduced-order models for complex turbulent flows. I’ve also designed neural networks that act like an Engine Control Unit and govern the functioning of combustion engines as well as AI pitch control for wind turbines.

Previously, I worked at the DynFluid Laboratory in Paris, France, where I focused on numerical simulations and data-driven methods for fluid dynamics. Prior to that, I was at the Institut AéroTechnique, conducting experimental wind tunnel testing and fluid simulations.

I’m passionate about applying smart algorithms to physical systems, bridging the gap between theory, computation, and real-world testing.
